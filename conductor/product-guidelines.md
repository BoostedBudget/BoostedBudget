# Product Guidelines: BoostedBudget

## 1. Design Philosophy
- **Approachable & Fun:** The interface should feel welcoming and encouraging, removing the stress often associated with personal finance. Use vibrant but professional colors, rounded corners, and subtle animations to create a delightful experience.
- **Visual Hierarchy:** Prioritize clarity. Key actions (like moving money or checking a budget) should be instantly recognizable.

## 2. Voice & Tone
- **Concise & Functional:** While the visual design is fun, the copy must be clear and direct. Avoid jargon and long explanations. Users should know exactly what happened and what to do next.
- **Encouraging:** Use positive reinforcement (e.g., "Great job hitting your goal!") but keep it brief and genuine.

## 3. User Experience (UX) Priorities
- **Onboarding Focus:** The first session is critical. Users must be able to connect an account and see their "Boosted" potential within minutes.
- **Daily Utility:** Optimized for quick check-ins. The mobile experience should allow users to categorize a transaction or approve a rollover in under 30 seconds.
- **Data Depth:** While the surface is simple, power users should be able to drill down into trends and historical data without cluttering the main dashboard.

## 4. Accessibility & Inclusivity
- **Standard Compliance:** Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. ensure sufficient contrast and screen reader compatibility.
- **Device Agnostic:** seamless experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
