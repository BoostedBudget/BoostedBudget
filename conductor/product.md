# Product Guide: BoostedBudget

## 1. Vision
BoostedBudget is a smart personal finance application designed to turn budgeting into proactive wealth building. Unlike traditional trackers that simply categorize past spending, BoostedBudget empowers users with "rollover rules" and actionable insights. Its core philosophy is to identify unspent funds in budget categories and intelligently guide users to manually move that surplus into savings or investment accounts, accelerating their financial goals.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Power Users:** Individuals who demand granular control over their financial data and automation rules.
- **Passive Savers:** Users who want a "set and forget" system that monitors their budget and alerts them when savings opportunities arise.
- **Families:** Households managing shared finances who need collaborative budgeting tools and goal tracking.

## 3. Core Features & Value Proposition
- **Smart Budgeting:** Automated categorization and spending insights that go beyond simple tracking.
- **Goal Tracking:** Visual progress bars for debt payoff, emergency funds, and major purchases.
- **Rollover Logic (The "Boost"):** A unique rules engine that detects under-budget categories at the end of a cycle and **prompts users** to transfer surplus funds to designated savings/investment accounts.
- **Proactive Savings:** Shifts the focus from "not spending" to "actively saving" by suggesting transfers and tracking the accumulated "boosted" savings over time.

## 4. Monetization Strategy
- **Premium Subscription:** A paid SaaS model ensuring user privacy and data security.
- **Extended Trial:** A generous 2-month free trial to allow users to experience the full "rollover" cycle and see the value.
- **Referral Program:** Incentivized growth where users earn 2 additional free months for every successful paid referral.
- **No Ads/Data Selling:** Strict commitment to user privacy; revenue comes solely from subscriptions.

## 5. Differentiation
- **Automation First:** Superior custom rules engine compared to competitors.
- **Action-Oriented:** Doesn't just report on the past; drives future wealth by notifying users of savings opportunities.
- **Customizability:** Flexible budgeting periods, category structures, and rule definitions to fit any financial lifestyle.
